About TrendIQ

Revolutionizing stock market predictions with artificial intelligence

🚀

Our Mission

TrendIQ is dedicated to democratizing access to advanced financial analysis tools. We believe that everyone should have access to sophisticated AI-powered market predictions, not just institutional investors.

🤖

The Technology

Our platform uses Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) neural networks, a type of deep learning architecture specifically designed for time-series prediction. These models analyze 60 days of historical stock data to predict next-day closing prices.

📊

How It Works

We collect historical stock data, preprocess it using advanced normalization techniques, and train LSTM models on patterns and trends. The trained models can then predict future prices with remarkable accuracy.
