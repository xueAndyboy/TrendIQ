Quantitative Methodology & Evaluation

A transparent breakdown of the time-series model architecture, evaluation protocols, and known constraints.

📐

Mathematical Formulation

TrendIQ formulates stock price projection as a regularized autoregressive time-series problem. Features comprise past price lags (\(y_{t-1} \dots y_{t-5}\)), rolling 5-day and 20-day moving averages, and historical rolling volatility. Ridge \(L_2\) regularization minimizes collinearity across correlated lag features.

🛡️

Leakage-Free Validation

Standard random k-fold cross-validation causes catastrophic lookahead bias in financial time series. TrendIQ enforces an 80/20 chronological time-based split, training exclusively on historical data and evaluating strictly on out-of-sample future test windows.

🎯

Naive Baseline Benchmarking

Under the Random Walk Hypothesis, tomorrow's price is best predicted by today's price (\(\hat{y}_{t+1} = y_t\)). TrendIQ compares all models directly against this Naive Persistence baseline and 20-day Moving Averages using RMSE, MAE, and MAPE.

📈 Multi-Horizon Uncertainty Modeling

Deterministic single-line predictions in finance are unrealistic because market volatility compounds over time. For multi-step forecasts (\(h \in [1, 30]\)), TrendIQ models predictive variance as expanding with the square root of time:

$$\text{Confidence Interval}(h) = \hat{y}_{t+h} \pm z \cdot \sigma_{\text{res}} \sqrt{h}$$

Where \(\sigma_{\text{res}}\) is the historical residual standard error of the fitted model, and \(z = 1.28\) for the 80% confidence band and \(z = 1.96\) for the 95% confidence band. Over longer horizons (e.g. 30 days), an exponential mean-reversion factor dampens runaway momentum.

📰 News Sentiment NLP Pipeline

Technical indicators alone fail to account for breaking macroeconomic and company-specific news. TrendIQ ingests live RSS news feeds from Yahoo Finance for the target ticker and runs an NLP financial sentiment scoring pipeline:

  • Tokenization & Lemmatization: Extracts keywords from article headlines.
  • Domain Lexicon Matching: Evaluates positive terms (e.g., surge, profit, beat, rally, dividend) vs negative terms (e.g., plunge, miss, drop, lawsuit, warning).
  • Normalized Polarity Score: Computes a continuous signal from -1.00 (maximum bearish) to +1.00 (maximum bullish).

⚠️ Known Limitations & Theoretical Boundaries

  • Efficient Market Hypothesis (EMH): Public historical prices are largely reflected in current quotes. High-frequency alpha cannot be captured via daily closing prices alone.
  • Black Swan Events: Statistical models assume stationarity in distribution tails and cannot anticipate geopolitical disruptions, sudden regulatory interventions, or unforeseen macroeconomic shocks.
  • Execution Slippage & Fees: Backtested accuracy does not account for bid-ask spreads, liquidity constraints, transaction fees, or market impact.
  • Educational Purpose: TrendIQ is designed strictly as an engineering and machine learning portfolio project. It is not financial advice or a registered investment system.
